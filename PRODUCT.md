# Product

## Register

product

## Users

1. **Dispatchers & Operations Managers**: Coordinating residential and commercial cleaning crews across the Greater Toronto Area (GTA), monitoring live job progress, managing recurring service schedules, and resolving unassigned dispatches.
2. **Account Executives & Field Sales Reps**: Canvassing residential and commercial territories via KnockLog, managing inbound leads, quoting services, and running B2B phone outreach.
3. **Field Coordinators & Workforce Managers**: Onboarding cleaners, reviewing applications, auditing timesheets, and reconciling cleaning supplies and equipment.
4. **Managing Partners & Finance Leadership**: Tracking unit economics, QuickBooks Online (QBO) sync, cleaner payroll, vendor payouts, and compliance/audit logs.

## Product Purpose

Sea of Blue OS is the central dispatch, sales, workforce, and financial operating system for Sea of Blue, a tech-enabled residential and commercial cleaning platform in Ontario. Success means every role lands immediately in a focused, high-density workspace tailored to their daily job without cognitive clutter from unrelated departments.

## Brand Personality

Commanding, Crisp, Operational. A fast, trustworthy multi-workspace operating system inspired by Linear, Raycast, and Stripe where navigation is effortless and operational state is unmistakable at a glance.

## Anti-references

- Bloated flat admin templates where 10+ unrelated departments sit in a single unorganized list.
- Oversized decorative logos or marketing fluff inside high-frequency operational tools.
- Disjointed sub-apps that feel like separate websites when switching between CRM, Dispatch, and Sales OS.

## Design Principles

1. **Role-Focused Workspaces over Flat Menus**: Group tools by the job being done (Dispatch & Ops, Sales & CRM, Workforce & Supply, Finance & Admin) so users only see the 4–5 surfaces relevant to their active workflow.
2. **Instant Context Switching**: Switching between workspaces or launching full-viewport workstations (Sales OS, Live Dispatch Map) should happen in one click or keystroke (`⌘1`–`⌘4`) without losing orientation.
3. **High-Signal Operational Density**: Prioritize scannable queues, live status badges, and actionable controls over decorative widgets or nested cards.
4. **Seamless Desktop-to-Field Ergonomics**: Desktop operators get keyboard-first collapsible rails and dense tables; mobile and tablet operators get touch-safe drawers and responsive action bars.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA contrast across dark and light operational surfaces.
- Full keyboard navigation for workspace switching (`⌘1`–`⌘4`, `[` to toggle sidebar, `Esc` to close drawers).
- Touch-target minimums (44×44px) on mobile/tablet navigation and field controls.
